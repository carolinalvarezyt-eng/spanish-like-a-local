Questions

Before you ask

Something else? Book a free call and ask me anything.

I'm a complete beginner. Is this for me?

Yes. The 12-week path starts from zero. You'll speak in the first session, even if it's only your name and where you're from.

I already speak some Spanish. Do I start at week 1?

No. Take the level test and book your call: your result comes with the booking. We skip what you know and I build the 12 weeks from your level.

What levels do you teach?

From complete beginner to advanced. The free level test places you in one of four levels (Beginner, Elementary, Intermediate or Advanced) and your plan starts from there.

What results can I expect in 12 weeks?

The goal is a 15-minute conversation in Spanish without switching to English. How far you get depends on where you start and how much you practice between classes, so I won't promise fluency in three months. At week 8 we check your progress and adjust your plan.

How do you teach?

You talk from the first minute. I explain each rule in plain English in a couple of minutes, then you use it right away. I correct you without stopping the conversation, and the corrections go into your notebook.

What does a class look like?

Forty-five minutes in four parts: a warm-up in Spanish, one new topic with examples from your own life, practice in a real situation (a role-play or a conversation), and a wrap-up with corrections and your mission for the week.

How quickly will I notice progress?

You speak in your first class. Every week ends with a mission in real life, so you notice it the first time you order, text or chat in Spanish that week. How fast it goes depends on how much you practice between classes.

Can I choose topics based on my interests?

Yes. Your plan starts from your level and what you want Spanish for: travel, work, your partner's family, living in Medellín. Your local list of music, series and places follows your taste too.

Do I have to be in Medellín?

No. Classes are online on Google Meet, and the music and series challenges work from anywhere. When I'm in Medellín we can meet in person, at no extra cost.

How much time does it take outside class?

Plan on about twenty minutes a day. Most of it is things you'd enjoy anyway: a song, an episode, a voice note to me.

What if I travel or miss a session?

Tell me 24 hours before and we reschedule. Without notice, the session counts as used. You have 14 weeks to use your 24 sessions.

Can I change my schedule?

Yes. Tell me 24 hours before and we move the session to another time that works for both of us.

What happens if I don't complete the program?

You have 14 weeks to use your 24 sessions, so there is room for a trip or a busy week. If you pay monthly, each payment covers the month you are about to start.

What happens after the 12 weeks?

In week 12 we look at how far you've come and plan what comes next together.

How does Duo work?

You and one other person share every session, and each of you pays your own plan. It works best when you start at a similar level.

How much does it cost?

You get the price together with your personal plan, on the free 20-minute call. There's no commitment: you pay only when you decide to start.

How do I pay?

By PayPal, Wise or a secure payment link. You can pay month by month, each payment before that month's first session, or everything upfront.

What happens on the free call?

It's 20 minutes on Google Meet. After you book, send me a short voice note on WhatsApp: your level test result, a quick hello in Spanish (“Hola, me llamo...”) and why you want to learn. If you can't say it in Spanish yet, English is fine. On the call we get to know each other, I answer your questions and you decide if it's for you.
